# Compact release validation - 24 November 2026

## What was checked

- The independent workstation test installed the pinned environments, ran all
  35 default plot stages and matched all 82 matching-release reference outputs.
- The simulation example passed: the complete and reduced snapshots produced
  the same 35,603 prepared cells.
- All 38 lettered main-figure panels were compared against the manuscript at
  plot-content or assembled-asset level. Figures 1-3 and supplementary figure
  files are unchanged in the final manuscript source update.
- The sole confirmed main-figure numerical discrepancy was the simulated AT+
  point and its SD bars in the assembled Figure 4j. The author corrected the
  artwork. The updated Figure 4 asset and compiled manuscript now agree with
  the deposited mean/SD plot, including all other points and SD bars.
- An independent calculation from the saved daily simulation count overview
  reproduced the cohort selection, failure/censoring limits and all 175
  per-run burden/treatment rows. For AT+ (34 runs), normalized burden is
  1.3123296589875786 +/- 0.03399013347091135 (sample SD); treatment fraction is
  0.29517879647926488 +/- 0.013538624088306443.

The final Figure 4j check compared 76 colored marker/error-bar paths. Maximum
coordinate residuals were 0.000666 pt in the source asset and 0.002993 pt in
the compiled manuscript. The scientific code, deposited values and comparison
policy were not changed. No further main-figure numerical mismatch was found
within this comparison scope.

## Limits and remaining publication tasks

This is plot reconstruction and manuscript comparison, not an independent
rerun of the entire raw-cohort analysis. Schematics, assembled layouts and
simulation image halves were checked as manuscript assets rather than being
regenerated by the compact route. One-pixel raster/export differences and
CSV floating-point differences around 1e-16 are not scientific discrepancies.
Earlier map-PDF comparison warnings in the archived validation records were
investigated; the intended 120 micrometre manuscript content corresponds.

The optional CZI demonstration remains unvalidated to completion. Its bounded
CPU attempt stopped after 20 minutes during the second channel; the first
channel produced 952 resistant labels versus 931 in the historical result.
Do not claim that fresh segmentation reproduces the historical measurements.
The default plot workflow does not use this fresh segmentation.

Some deposited CSV provenance strings retain author build paths; these are
not reviewer input paths. Before publication, supply the final licence,
version/DOI and data/code links, and reconcile the manuscript availability
statements with the compact deposit's stated starting boundary.
